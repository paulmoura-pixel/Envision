Jonathan Conceicao:

Observation Statement:
40% of users identified that too many ads or paid features as the primary reason they would stop using a fitness app. This demonstrates that ads and restrictive payment models may negatively affect user satisfaction and retention.

Supporting Data:
Of the survey participants, 40% selected “Too many ads or paid features” as their biggest reason for stopping or potentially stopping use of a fitness app, suggesting that minimizing advertisements and restrictions on essential features could improve the user experience.


Team Action (if any):
We plan to make Envision an add free app, allowing users to use it without interruptions.
Instead of relying on ads or charging clients for essential features, we plan to implement a coach-based subscription model, where fitness coaches pay a fee based on the number of clients they manage, and the coaches would have the option to collect payments independently or through Envision. Payments processed through our platform could generate additional revenue through transaction fees.
Eliminating ads would also reduce our reliance on internet as most ads normally run over the internet to be able to dynamically display them, and supporting our goal of providing a seamless offline experience.
